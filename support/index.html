Support

The W App · Last updated 30 September 2026

Write to duok@duokteknoloji.com with anything at all — a bug, a whisky the app gets wrong, or an idea. We answer within a few days, and we read every message.

Things people ask

The scan picked the wrong whisky

Tap "No — it's a different bottle" and either choose from the list or add the whisky yourself. Telling us which bottle it was (a photo helps) is the fastest way to get it fixed for everyone.

My whisky isn't in the catalogue

Add it: the "+" button, then "Add manually", or "Add it" after a scan. Only the name is needed. What you add becomes part of the shared catalogue, without your name on it.

I didn't get my sign-up or reset code

Check your spam folder first, and tell us if it was there — that helps us fix it. Codes expire after an hour; "Send a new code" issues another.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. It removes your tastings, notes, wishlist, collection, photos and scan history immediately. Whiskies you added to the shared catalogue stay, with nothing linking them to you. See the privacy policy.

Something in the catalogue is wrong

Open the whisky and use "Report a problem", or write to us.
