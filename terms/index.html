Bottle recognition can be wrong

Identifying a bottle from a photo is done by an AI system and can make mistakes. The app always asks you to confirm before anything is saved. Facts looked up on the web are marked as suggestions for you to check. Always rely on the bottle's own label.

Your content

There are two different things you can do with a photo in this app, and they are treated differently. The difference is the whole of this section.

  • A photo on your own tasting stays yours and stays private. So do your tastings and notes. We store them to show back to you, and show them to other people only if you mark that tasting public. They are deleted with your account, and you can delete any of them at any time before that.
  • A photo you add as a whisky’s picture in the shared catalogue is a contribution to something everyone uses. When you add a photo of a bottle to a whisky in our shared catalogue, you grant us a worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual licence to use it in The W App, including as that whisky’s picture shown to other users. The photo is used without your name, and it stays in the catalogue even if you delete your account — though you can always ask us to remove a specific photo: duok@duokteknoloji.com
  • The licence covers showing it, not training on it. We do not use your photos to train artificial-intelligence models, and neither does the provider that reads bottle labels for us. The reading is recognition only: it tells us what the label says, and learns nothing from the picture. The same is true of your tastings and notes.
  • If you would rather a photo did not stay, you can remove it before you close your account: open the whisky, tap Edit, and remove or replace the picture. Whiskies you added, and their wordings, stay in the catalogue either way — without your name — because other people’s tastings are attached to them.
  • Only upload photos you took or have the right to share, and nothing unlawful, offensive, or about other people without their consent.
