WHISKY TASTING JOURNAL

The W App

Taste. Remember. Discover.

  • Point your camera at a bottle and the app works out what it is, then asks you to confirm. It never writes a guess into your journal.
  • Record a tasting in a minute — a score, a few words, whether you would drink it again.
  • Keep three lists apart: what you have tasted, what you want, and what you own.
  • Private by default. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold. Delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.
